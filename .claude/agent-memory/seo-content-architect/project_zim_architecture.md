## Key Cannibalization Risks

1. /algonquin-student-rentals-ottawa vs /algonquin-college-off-campus-housing-guide — both target "Algonquin College off-campus housing" and "student rentals near Algonquin College." Both share the same hero image and same property cards.
2. /student-housing-resources-ottawa vs /resources — names overlap but serve genuinely different audiences (/resources is tenant-facing operational; /student-housing-resources-ottawa is SEO hub for prospects). Not true cannibalization but risks confusing crawlers if both rank for "student housing resources Ottawa."
3. /algonquin-student-rentals-ottawa vs /student-rentals-nepean-baseline-station — both are commercial transactional, same hero image, same PropertyCard listings, same $850 price, overlapping keyword sets. Differentiation is thin.
4. /ottawa-student-housing-cost-guide vs /furnished-student-room-vs-apartment-ottawa — both cover "total monthly cost" comparison with nearly identical table structures. Overlap is moderate.

## Thin / Templated Content Flags

- /parents-student-housing-ottawa — generic Q&A, no parent-specific information gain over /algonquin-student-rentals-ottawa. The "questions parents usually ask" section repeats data visible elsewhere on the homepage.
- /international-student-housing-ottawa — weakest page in the set. Adds a checklist and "why furnished rooms help" section but no content unique to international students (visa process, study permit housing requirements, SIN, bank setup). Functionally a reskin of /parents.
- /student-rentals-nepean-baseline-station — all content about the "Baseline Station" differentiator is hedged ("at or near"). The page's benefits, property cards, and conversion CTAs are identical to /algonquin-student-rentals-ottawa.
- Blog: cornerstoneBlogPosts are appropriately substantive (especially healthy-eating post). Five cornerstone posts are generic enough to target real search queries. Monthly posts add real neighbourhood specificity.

## Internal Linking Gaps

- Blog posts (all 5 cornerstone posts) have NO internal links back to any commercial landing page — they are effectively orphaned from the conversion funnel.
- /resources has NO outbound links to any SEO landing page or blog post.
- /blog page does not link to /student-housing-resources-ottawa hub.
- Homepage HomepageSeoSections uses anchor links (#location-advantage etc.) rather than linking to the SEO landing pages (/algonquin-student-rentals-ottawa, /student-rentals-nepean-baseline-station).

## Missing Proof / Trust Signals

- No testimonials or social proof on any page.
- No author byline on Article-schema pages (uses Organization as author — weaker for E-E-A-T).
- No explicit walktime/transit time to Algonquin College with source (hedged throughout as "near" or "close").

## "Broad Head Term" Coverage

No page explicitly targets "student housing Ottawa" as primary keyword. The homepage title leads with "Ottawa Student Rentals Near Algonquin College" — that's a good long-tail frame but the broad head term is unclaimed. /student-housing-resources-ottawa is the closest match but is positioned as a hub, not a commercial page.

## Why: Content audit requested June 2026 to identify cannibalization, thin content, and missing links before further content investment.
## How to apply: Use findings to prioritize differentiation of /algonquin vs /baseline pages, add blog-to-landing-page links, and assess whether /international and /parents pages need genuine audience-specific content or consolidation.
